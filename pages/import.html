🏦 Bank Statement Import

Import transactions from a bank or M-Pesa statement. Link each transaction to a customer, supplier or expense before saving.

Supports Excel and CSV. Saving records each line once: a customer payment lowers that customer's balance and adds money to the account, a supplier payment lowers what you owe and takes money out, and an expense goes to Expenses. Product stock is not changed.

You can edit transaction details and choose the customer, supplier, product, or expense category before saving the imported statement. Do not import a payment you have already recorded by hand, or it will be counted twice.
